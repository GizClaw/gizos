# AMOLED 聚气对决气氛稿提示词 v1

生成方式：Codex 内置 ImageGen。

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: early concept mockup for a 368 x 448 portrait AMOLED embedded-game screen
Primary request: visualize a compact one-screen game inspired by a childhood simultaneous hand-clap battle with exactly three actions: defend, gather energy, and fire an energy wave.
Scene/backdrop: near-black AMOLED background with a subtle continuously moving sci-fi training arena, layered distant speed lines and drifting particles.
Subject: centered first-person battle view; a stylized opponent fighter stands in the upper-middle distance; the player's two forearms/hands enter from the lower left and lower right, framing the action area.
Composition/framing: exact portrait aspect ratio 368:448. The bottom quarter contains a curved carousel selector: one large centered circular skill icon and two smaller neighboring skill icons fading toward the left and right edges. Along the upper rim of the carousel is an arc of exactly ten small segmented energy cells, with four lit. Keep the center 55% of the screen clear for action animation. Include a tiny player health indicator at top left and opponent indicator at top right, but no words.
Style/medium: polished low-fidelity game UI concept, crisp 2D arcade interface, readable at smartwatch-like size, slight 1990s fighting-game energy but entirely original, no copied characters, no logos.
Lighting/mood: luminous cyan, electric violet, and warm orange accents on true black; high contrast suitable for AMOLED.
Constraints: no readable text, no watermark, no phone frame, no hands holding the device, no extra buttons, exactly three selectable skill icons represented by shield, energy orb, and projectile-wave symbols, exactly ten energy segments, interface must feel feasible on low-power embedded hardware.
```

说明：气氛稿用于确定层级、颜色和第一人称构图，不是可直接切图的量产 UI。几何位置与 10 格气槽以 SVG 线框图为准。
