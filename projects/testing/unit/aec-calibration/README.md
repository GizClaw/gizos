# AEC calibration

`//projects/testing/unit/aec-calibration/app:aec_calibration` is a headless App reusable by independent product firmware entries. It searches caller-supplied speaker-volume and microphone-gain combinations through Audio, Memory and Time PAL, requiring working AEC, acceptable clipping/headroom, near-end retention and processing cadence. The App does not implement AEC or depend on a board, codec, ESP-SR, WakeNet, GizClaw, network or production Main. This is a single-DUT suite; the independent playback source is an acoustic fixture, not a second DUT.

## Composition and evidence

The caller supplies exclusive, stopped Audio PAL devices, candidates, thresholds and an independent near-end source callback. Optional Audio PAL diagnostics supply raw ADC, actual AEC reference input and processed PCM for the same frame, and independently observe the mixed PCM actually handed to DAC transport. The provider and board own the SDK, codec-input-to-packed-lane mapping, reference gain and analog limits. Missing real AEC, pre/post diagnostics or an independent near-end source returns `UNSUPPORTED`; simulated data, microphone silence, fixture EOF and low output energy cannot establish success.

The near-end callback must loop the App's mono S16LE probe through a real acoustic source outside the DUT and acknowledge start/stop. It cannot inject PCM by overriding DUT `mic_read` or use the DUT's own speaker as the near-end source. Source control has an explicit finite timeout, five seconds per call by default. The runner records call counts, total duration and maximum duration and rejects late completion, but cannot preempt a callback that never returns. The caller fixes and records distance, angle, source level, device pose, environment, fixture version and DUT image/board/UID. The borrowed probe buffer remains alive until the independent source stops successfully. Products without the fixture may explicitly return `UNSUPPORTED`; host fakes do not establish physical calibration.

Speaker and microphone controls use the provider's 0–100 range. Microphone 0% is a legal minimum gain and is not assumed silent; speaker 0% is excluded from optimization. Every request is read back immediately, and reports preserve requested and actual values. Pareto and recommendation comparisons use measured actual values. The App cannot exceed the board's codec gain, supply or reference-input contract; ES7210 microphone adjustments preserve the independent reference-input gain.

## Measurements

Each candidate runs noise, far-only, near-only, double-talk and sustained double-talk at two amplitude levels. Every phase stops and joins DUT Audio independently, then warms up again before scoring. Default single-tone amplitudes are 2048 and 4096; the theoretical peak of three summed tones is at most three times the single-tone amplitude, which does not represent full-scale speech stress. Default far frequencies are approximately 437, 1031 and 2156 Hz; near frequencies are approximately 719, 1438 and 2938 Hz. Actual frequencies are `bin × sample_rate / frame_samples` and are reported with the format. Formats that cannot provide six distinct usable bins are rejected.

Defaults are 64 warmup frames, 96 ordinary scoring frames and 640 sustained scoring frames, approximately 2.05, 3.07 and 20.48 seconds at 16 kHz and 512 samples. Each PAL I/O has a finite timeout. Timing starts before microphone startup, covers all warmup and measurement frames, and extends through the final actual capture/DAC completion. The lower bound includes every frame, allowing only 1 ms of monotonic-clock sampling granularity. Total time must stay within 125% of real PCM duration plus 100 ms; long per-frame timeouts cannot disguise low throughput as sustained real-time processing. Reports retain frames, samples and actual `elapsed_ms`. Provider software sequences verify diagnostic delivery order, not lossless hardware ADC capture; platforms without hardware drop counters receive only the processing-cadence qualification measurable here.

Every scoring frame requires one complete successful actual DAC callback, not a partial window. Every far-only, double-talk and sustained frame must contain nonzero PCM. DUT PCM must be silent during noise and near-only phases; zero-filled missing probes cannot pass.

All phases check raw microphone, raw reference, scaled AEC reference, AEC output and actual DAC PCM peak/headroom. The default peak limit is 30000; reaching it is a rejection, and clipping statistics include this conservative headroom violation. Far-only requires active reference, raw microphone signal sufficiently above noise and at least 10 dB of background-subtracted echo-power suppression by default, checked independently for all three far bands. Double-talk and sustained phases use the same-level near-only far-bin energy as background, require actual far-band echo input within 0.5–2 times the far-only input, and require the same suppression. Retaining near-end signal while passing far-end echo cannot pass. Every near band must be active in near-only and retain at least 25% of raw power by default. Double-talk and sustained phases must retain at least 50% of near-only output power and verify independent-source input stability. Total energy cannot substitute for individual near-band evidence.

Digital DAC PCM and raw ADC headroom do not establish analog amplifier/speaker distortion performance.

These controlled, separated-frequency probes are easier than overlapping speech. Success applies only to this fixture, input level, thresholds and measured candidates. It does not establish absolute dB SPL, a global maximum across continuous gain settings, speech intelligibility, strong-wind behavior or long-term acoustic qualification. Higher content peaks require new stress amplitudes/headroom and measurements. Real near-end speech, overlapping double-talk, endurance and product self-dialogue behavior need separate verification; output in three bands cannot establish those results.

## Results and cleanup

All measured candidates and failure evidence are retained. `PARETO_ONLY` returns nondominated usable combinations. `SPEAKER_FIRST` maximizes speaker volume, then microphone gain at that volume; `MIC_FIRST` reverses the order. Identical actual values retain input order. A tradeoff between directions does not imply a unique simultaneous maximum. Results are never automatically applied to a product or persisted.

The runner allocates through Memory PAL, copies candidates and limits, and borrows provider and independent-source user data. Callbacks do not wait, allocate or call Audio. Capture and playback write separate counters, which the runner reads only after stop and join. Registering or removing an observer requires both workers stopped; failure retains the previous registration user. A phase, stop, close, unregister or control-restoration failure prevents qualification. Cleanup stops and joins DUT microphone/speaker workers before stopping the independent source. DUT stop or track-close failure retains the independent source, borrowed probe and dependencies for retry. Track-close failure also retains its handle and speaker to prevent retries through an address invalidated by provider destruction. If stopping the independent source fails after DUT workers stop, the probe remains until that source stops successfully. Failed cleanup retains the runner, probe and dependencies for `cleanup` or `destroy` retries until success.

`h2_aec_calibration_report()` writes `AEC_CALIBRATION` JSON records through the caller's writer with a consistent run identity. Records include format, probe, amplitudes, frequencies, thresholds, requested/actual controls, complete per-phase measurements, Pareto, selection, completion and cleanup. The writer must verify complete writes and append newlines. Probe identity `three-band-two-level-v2` binds the double-talk per-far-band suppression contract; older probe records cannot inherit this qualification. The independent public validator rechecks phases, energy, clipping, per-band far suppression, near retention, cadence, Pareto and selection, rejecting missing, duplicate, mixed-run or contradictory PASS records.

```sh
bazel run --config=macos_arm64 \
  //projects/testing/unit/aec-calibration/tools:report -- \
  --log /absolute/path/aec-calibration.log --run <16-digit-hex-run>
```

Exit code 0 means a complete report contains at least one fully qualified candidate for this probe; 1 means a valid report with no fully qualified candidate; 2 means invalid or incomplete data. The report alone does not establish physical device or fixture identity. Retain original logs, package/image SHA, actual provider, independent source and placement records.

## Validation

`//projects/testing/unit/aec-calibration/app:calibration_test` uses independent fakes to check candidate selection, both priority policies, Pareto, minimum microphone gain, quantized readback, silence, double-talk over-suppression, absent echo cancellation, echo leakage confined to double-talk or sustained phases, missing/clipped reference, malformed format, fast/slow clocks, and retention/retry after resource failures. `//projects/testing/unit/aec-calibration/tools:report_test` runs the same C runner/formatter and independently validates records, rejecting tampered near, far, reference, clipping, cadence, Pareto, selection and run identity. Host results establish execution and rejection contracts; each real provider/product still requires native package compilation and physical fixture evidence.
