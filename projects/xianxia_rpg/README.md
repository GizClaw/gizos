# Xianxia RPG

《竹溪仙缘》 is an original top-down xianxia role-playing game in Chinese. Version 0.7 bundles seven chapters: 《竹溪灯影》, where the hero seals the ink dragon that darkened the creek; 《渡口迷雾》, where he lights lamp posts with Jiang Li to clear the river fog; 《雨夜剑阁》, where the ferry's sword tassel leads them up a rainy mountain to rotate sword posts, read three teaching steles and face the sword spirit Hearing Rain; and 《万灯归山》, where the pair return to the village, escort fire through ridge winds, rekindle three lamps and defend the altar together; followed by 《照水旧城》, where tide bells open different routes through a flooded city, a duel ends without a death and a rescue interrupts the feud; and 《百草寒谷》, where either a warm spring path or a sliding ice creek yields medicine for Jiang Li, followed by a step-based healing ritual; and 《镜湖问心》, where paired past/present routes reveal the original two-way lantern pact, three choices change Jiang Li's replies, and a heart shadow repeats the previous accepted battle command. Like [Fruit Slice](../fruit_slice/), the portable App in [`app/`](app/) depends only on Runtime and the Display, Touch and Pref PAL contracts; it renders RGB565 in software and pushes only the dirty row bands each frame. The public contract is [`h2_xianxia_rpg.h`](app/include/h2_xianxia_rpg.h).

## Playing

Every action is a tap or mouse click. Tap the ground to walk along the shortest path; tap a character, the well, a lamp post or a boat to walk over and interact; tap anywhere to advance dialogue. A gold diamond marks whom or what to visit next, the HUD shows HP, MP, pills and the current objective, and a banner announces every objective change. Battles offer 攻击, 剑诀 (three strikes for 7 MP, taught by the Daoist), 丹药, 防御 (softens the next blow and restores MP) and 逃跑. The chapter-1 boss telegraphs 墨浪 with a 蓄力 turn, roars at low health and then charges more often; guarding through the wave is the intended answer. Losing a battle revives the hero at the last rest point with full strength; losing to a boss also explains how to recover.

In chapter 2 the companion acts after the hero, and the bubble above her shows what she will do: she lifts her lantern to strip an enemy's veil (a veiled enemy takes 30% damage), braces a guarding hero, or strikes with her pole. Unveiled hits, her lantern and her bracing each light one of three rapport lanterns; with all three lit, 剑诀 becomes 合击, a joint strike that also breaks a charge. The boss 雾魇 veils itself, gathers a mist lock that seals sword arts for one command, and below half health splits into two shadows: after choosing a strike, tap the shadow her lantern rings. The tall reeds hide ambushes, and paths avoid them when another way exists. Lit lamp posts are rest points.

Chapter 3 continues from the chapter-2 result with its level, pills, trust and clues. Without that clear it offers a recap and Lv6, three pills, the sword art and Jiang Li. Both earlier chapters remain replayable. The west, south and east sword posts turn clockwise on each tap; point their bright tips east, north and west toward the central stele. The gate then exposes the third teaching stele. Each stele enables its matching battle hint: guard through 垂雨 to fill rapport, use the joint strike to interrupt 穿檐, and guard or take a pill during 回风 to avoid its counter. The three poses repeat without a time limit. Safe stone paths avoid all optional encounters; the default hero can win without grinding or pills. Every map has a rest point and the hall establishes one automatically before the boss.

Chapter 4 continues from the chapter-3 result or a short recap with Lv7 and three pills. Ask Tao Bo, then take fire from the source lamp beside the village well (column 8, row 4, zero-based). The bottom Jiang Li button switches between scouting and protecting the fire; protection makes wind tiles safe. Each completed step onto a blue wind tile checks the mode, pauses the route and gives feedback. Waiting never changes the outcome. An extinguished flame returns the party to the last lit lamp with a fresh flame and full strength; previously lit lamps remain. Light the east ridge, west ridge and altar lamps in order. The altar then becomes the retry checkpoint; tap its third lamp again to begin the defense.

The final defense has an 80-point lantern and three waves. Survive three enemy actions per wave or defeat that wave early. The next target is shown before choosing: lamp, party member, then a heavy lamp strike. 护灯 replaces escape, intercepts lamp damage at half strength and restores MP; ordinary 防御 only protects the party. Jiang Li has health derived from the saved hero level. Tap her status box to switch between a pole strike and restoring 12 lantern durability; selecting the tactic spends no turn. 丹药 waits for a tap on either party status box and can revive her. Her defeat alone is not a loss, and she recovers one HP after victory. The hero falling or the lamp going out retries all three waves at full lantern durability. The default party can win with no pills or extra encounters. After victory, return to the village to hear the downstream news, recover the upper half of the old lamp register and depart for chapter 5.

Chapter 5 continues from the fourth ending or starts at Lv8 with three pills. Ask the doctor at the floating market, then go east. In the old city, low tide exposes the brown street to the page at (11,7); high tide opens the teal roof bridge to the tower at the upper right. Tap the gold bell at (3,3) to switch tides and save on the permanent stone landing at (3,4). The market bell also switches the shared tide. Both tides leave the bells and their landings accessible. The optional 潮獭 button starts a water foe whose attack is 20% stronger at high tide; it is never required to progress.

Bring the page to Wen's grandmother and answer with one of the two bottom buttons. The answer is carried for later dialogue, without blocking progress. Tap Wen Zhou for a duel: survive six enemy turns or reduce him to half HP; damage is capped there so he never dies. He uses Hearing Rain's three stances, with hints enabled by the carried chapter-3 steles. Victory saves at the old-city bell with a flood in progress. Ring low, follow the street to grandmother at (11,6), then carry her back within fourteen completed steps. Ring high to reconnect the evacuation bridge and leave west for the market. Waiting, dialogue and refreshing do not spend steps. An exhausted budget returns everyone to the bell and saves a fresh rescue attempt, retaining the evidence and duel result. The pickup, tide switches, safe arrival and ending are checkpoints. Talk to the doctor to finish the chapter and open the next thread, 百草寒谷. Fifth-chapter dialogue pages continue on additional taps at small display sizes.

Chapter 6 continues directly from the fifth ending, or offers a recap with Lv9 and three pills. Talk to Xun Qiniang in the herb hut, then choose either route with the bottom buttons (or the west/east exit). Either medicine is sufficient. Jiang Li follows but cannot fight until recovered. There are no random fights or real-time limits in this chapter: its cold and heat rules belong to exploration and the ritual. Temperature starts at four; medicine can be gathered at two through six. At zero or eight the medicinal rope returns the party safely to the last spring/fire checkpoint, with the medicine retained. Waiting never changes temperature.

On the warm route, each completed step onto a red steam tile adds two heat. Tap a blue spring to cool to four and save. The short steam path has springs beside it; the longer lower stone path avoids steam entirely. Fire moss grows at (12,2). On the frozen route, tap a cell in the same row or column to slide in that direction until a pale stopping stone or a wall; diagonal taps do nothing. Each completed slide lowers temperature by one and saves, while golden fires restore four and establish checkpoints. The route is right, up, right; warm at the fire (11,0); down; warm at (11,7); right, up, and gather ice soul at (12,2). The bottom 回药庐 button returns safely from either route and allows switching routes. Taking both medicines is optional and grants the temperature jade, expanding the ritual's steady band from 3–5 to 2–6.

Return to the hut and tap the two-color spring at (7,1). Each ritual step previews its next breath: hot +2 calls for 凉药 (-2), cold -2 calls for 暖药 (+2), and an interfering spring spirit calls for 驱灵, which cancels that step's disturbance. 护心 holds both the meter and steady count for one step; it cannot itself complete the ritual. Five consecutive steady steps heal Jiang Li. Reaching zero or eight returns to a saved retry at the spring with medicine intact; 退回 also stops safely. Every command saves the meter, streak and step sequence, so refresh resumes the same choice. Medicine is prepared from the route's herb and does not spend pills. Recovery records the spring glyph and raises future rapport capacity to four while leaving inherited trust at 0–3. Xun identifies the technique as 以剑系灯 from the Shen family without prematurely confirming its maker; her directions and Wen Zhou's letter lead to chapter 7, 镜湖问心.

Chapter 7 continues from the sixth result or offers a recap with Lv9, three pills and the recovered companion's four-lantern capacity. The HUD always names the era. On the shore, tap the left 镜石 at (3,3) to enter the past, cross the middle wooden bridge, and help the child place a lamp at (11,3). The right mirror at (11,5) returns to the present: the lamp lights the lower passage, and 镜廊 at (14,6) enters the second map. Present and past use different routes through the same cells. Each mirror switches onto its permanent landing at (x,4), restores strength and saves; there is no timer or stranded landing.

In the mirror hall, turn the present water wheel at (5,3), switch to the past at (3,3), and take the upper ford to the ancient pact at (11,2). Clearing today's water reveals the old route; the story explicitly treats this as recovering the obscured image, rather than changing history. Three binary questions then use the two bottom buttons. Each answer saves immediately, changes trust by one within 0–3, and gives a distinct Jiang Li reply. Refresh during that reply replays it without applying trust a second time. Every answer leaves the story and routes open. The third answer can be recalled with 听江篱说; the earlier chapter-5 reply also changes her pre-question recollection.

After the questions, tap 心影 at (13,5). The shadow starts with guard, then repeats the previous accepted command: attack, three-strike sword art, two-strike joint attack, a 24-HP self-heal, or guard. Its next command is displayed before committing; unavailable commands do not change its memory. Guarding reduces its incoming damage by 30% and spends its action without an attack. Jiang Li lights the mirror or braces a guarding hero instead of passively damaging it. Alternate sword and guard to exploit the one-turn delay; the default hero can win without pills. A defeat saves a fresh attempt beside the right mirror with choices and paths intact; refreshing a battle also returns to the pre-battle checkpoint, not the middle of a turn. Victory saves before replayable revelations about the broken bridge, interrupted lantern exchange and an unnamed keeper entering the mountain. Today's two lantern teams appear endangered on Snow Ridge, leading to chapter 8 without prematurely confirming the keeper's identity.

The title offers 新的旅程 (chapter 1), 继续旅程 (the journey saved last) and 章节. The chapter screen resumes an unfinished chapter or starts it, with 从头 to restart it. Chapter 2 is always open: after a chapter-1 clear it starts with that hero, otherwise with a recap card and Lv4, three pills and the sword art. Finishing each chapter leads into the next playable chapter; the seventh ending returns to the title.

## Saves

All records live in the `xianxia_rpg` Pref namespace; the Web entry keeps them in browser `localStorage`, per origin, and the desktop entry keeps them in memory only. Defeats in chapters 1–5 revive without writing; chapter 7 saves the restored retry checkpoint. A chapter-5 rescue setback deliberately saves its new safe attempt.

| Key | Format | Written |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `save0`..`save3` | Chapter-1 journey, four `u32` exactly as v0.1 writes them; `save3` holds the statistics and may be absent | Elder's briefing, Daoist's teaching, well or spring, after the boss, at the chapter-1 ending |
| `journey` | 32-byte blob: a chapter-2 journey with its rest point, trust and a fingerprint of `save0`..`save3` at the time it was written | Chapter start, briefing, companion joining, tea stall, lit lamp posts, the boat, landing, after the guard and the wraith, returning to the dock, the chapter-2 ending |
| `journey3` | The same 32-byte v2 layout for chapter 3; its fingerprint covers both older journeys | Chapter start, letter, each post rotation, each stele, rest points, hall arrival, boss victory, reveal and ending |
| `journey4` | The same 32-byte v2 layout for chapter 4; its fingerprint covers `save0`..`save3`, `journey` and `journey3` | Chapter start, joining, fire source, escort mode, lit lamps, rest, defense victory, homecoming and ending |
| `journey5` | The same 32-byte v2 layout for chapter 5; its fingerprint also includes the raw `journey4` bytes | Start, briefing, page, bell/tide, answer, evidence, duel result, rescue pickup/retry/safe arrival, rest and ending |
| `journey6` | 36-byte v3 blob: the first 28 bytes follow v2, then a separate fact word and a checksum; predecessor fingerprint also includes raw `journey5` | Start, briefing, route selection/return, springs/fires, completed slides, herbs, thermal retreat, every ritual command, recovery and ending |
| `journey7` | Separate 36-byte v3 blob; predecessor fingerprint additionally includes the exact raw `journey6` bytes | Start, mirrors, lamp, water, pact, answers/feedback completion, pre-battle/retry, victory and ending |
| `chapters` | 168-byte blob: cleared-chapter bits and the hero at each clear | Every chapter ending when the table is readable; once at start when only `save0`..`save3` hold a chapter-1 clear |

Chapters 3–7 use separate journey keys because earlier releases reject journeys for unplayable chapters. The newest journey takes precedence only while the older journeys have not changed. Rolling back to v0.4 still opens its untouched fourth-chapter result; upgrading again resumes chapter 5. If the older build saves new play, Continue follows that play and the fifth-chapter row still resumes its own checkpoint. The ten-slot `chapters` layout and version stay unchanged: earlier builds already decode all ten slots. Startup imports a v0.1 clear only when the first completion slot is absent; it does not replace an existing earlier completion. Unreadable or future-version blobs are never replaced automatically, including during legacy migration. Explicit starts/checkpoints replace only their chapter's journey. Completing a chapter updates its slot in a readable completion table, preserving all other slots and their reserved bytes. An unreadable table remains untouched even at a new ending; that ending is still saved in its own journey. Except for chapter 7's saved retry, a battle defeat does not write, so consumed pills and turns remain in memory until another checkpoint. Checkpoints restore both party members. Chapter-5 flags keep tide/page in bits 0–1, remaining rescue steps in bits 4–7, the return-light answer in bit 30 and the rescued-grandmother/tide-glyph fact in bit 31. The fingerprint is the chapter-1 tag XOR the unchanged FNV fingerprints of each older journey, including the established empty-input fingerprint for absent records.

Chapter 6 keeps all prior carry flags intact and uses only the chapter-local low byte for temperature (bits 0–3) and steady count (4–6). Its separate facts word has fire moss (0), ice soul (1), first route cold (2), healed/spring glyph/four-lantern capacity (3), and first route chosen (4). It does not reuse any earlier carry-bit meaning. The low facts byte is written only into the sixth completion slot's reserved byte, which formal v0.5 already preserves; every other slot and reserved byte remains untouched. Unknown fact bits, versions, checksums and types are rejected without startup writes. Rolling back to v0.5 resumes its untouched fifth record; upgrading again resumes the sixth. If v0.5 actively saves new play, Continue follows that new fifth record and the sixth row retains its independent checkpoint. The v3 fingerprint uses the same XOR of raw predecessor fingerprints, including the established empty-input hash for absent keys. Trust stays within the old decoders' 0–3 range; four-lantern capacity derives from the healed fact.

Chapter 7 keeps the released `journey6` decoder and all earlier keys untouched. Facts bits 0–4 retain chapter-6 meaning; new bits 5–7 record the placed lamp, cleared water and read pact, bits 8–10 the three answers, and bit 11 the mirror glyph. Chapter-local flag bits 0–1 hold the era and pending answer feedback. Only the seventh completion slot's reserved byte receives the three answer bits plus the mirror-clear bit; the sixth slot and its route facts remain byte-for-byte unchanged. Unknown journey7 facts or local state, malformed/future records and read errors remain opaque. Automatic onward from the sixth ending resumes an existing seventh journey and returns to the title for an unreadable seventh record; only an explicit chapter entry can replace that chapter's record. On the same browser origin, v0.6 still reads and writes its own sixth journey and preserves the seventh slot. After old-build active play, Continue follows that new sixth progress while the seventh chapter row resumes its own checkpoint.

Continue resumes the `journey` record when its fingerprint matches the current `save0`..`save3`, and the chapter-1 record otherwise, so playing v0.1 again and returning still resumes what was saved last. The App never rewrites `save0`..`save3` for a later chapter, so a v0.1 build on the same origin keeps reading its own record. Blobs carry a checksum; a record that fails validation is ignored and left untouched, and the title says so.

## Content

Chapter 1 runs 竹溪村 → 幽篁小径 → 墨溪古祠 → back to 竹溪村; chapter 2 runs 青萍渡 → 芦苇滩 → 沙洲沉船 → back to 青萍渡; chapter 3 runs 雨中山道 → 废剑阵庭 → 剑阁正殿; chapter 4 reuses the original village layout at night, then visits 东脊 → 西脊 → 竹溪祭台 → back to the relit village. Chapter 1, the chapter table and the shared UI strings live in [`h2_xianxia_rpg_story.c`](app/src/h2_xianxia_rpg_story.c); each later chapter owns one file, such as [`h2_xianxia_rpg_chapter2.c`](app/src/h2_xianxia_rpg_chapter2.c), with its maps, characters, scripts and tile rules. Maps are 15x10 character grids, scripts are lists of speaker lines with one effect when they finish, and `h2_xianxia_rpg_talk_script()` chooses what each character says at each story stage. Stages run in play order across chapters. The story tables are also the glyph sources of the body font, which the build generates from the shared [Noto Sans SC](../../libs/ui/fonts/noto_sans_sc/) font. The bundled subset has no 「」 or 『』, so text uses “” and 《》; `render_test` fails when any on-screen string has a missing glyph.

The run loop logs `H2_XIANXIA_RPG_STATE` once per phase, map, stage, battle-state, dialogue-line, story-flag or walking change, `H2_XIANXIA_RPG_RECORDS_LOAD` and one store line per written record, and `H2_XIANXIA_RPG_STATS` every ten seconds with frame time and pushed pixels.
