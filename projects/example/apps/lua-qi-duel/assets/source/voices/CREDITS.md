The charge, wave, absorb and guard originals are retained; other recordings are brief excerpts. Mastering applies a 90 Hz
high-pass filter, edge fades and peak normalization; no voice cloning, pitch
shifting. All four skill cues are synthesized speech; hurt is a recording. Embedded PCM includes leading silence for timing.
